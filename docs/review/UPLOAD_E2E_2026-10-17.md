Notes, honestly:

* **MOT17-09 against its ground truth** (the only clip here with truth; computed afterwards with
  `eval/gate_events.py` on the same line scaled to 1920×1080, 15-frame match window): truth is
  **3 IN / 10 OUT** (26 people in the sequence); the upload counted **0 IN / 7 OUT**; 6 of the 7
  matched a true crossing within 15 frames, and 3 of those were attributed to the right person. 45
  track IDs for 26 people. The upload UI correctly does not show any of this (no ground truth for user
  clips); it is here so nobody reads the upload counts as accurate.
* **Shibuya 0 / 0 with a horizontal line is not a counting bug**: the people are close to the camera,
  so their box bottoms sit at y ≈ 660–720 of 720 (5th–95th percentile) and they walk sideways (median
  vertical travel 6 px for tracks > 30 frames); no foot point ever reached y = 395. With a vertical
  line the same video counts 0 / 4. UX gap (P1): the gate editor gives no hint where foot points are;
  showing the foot-point trails, or warning "no track crosses this line" after the run, would catch it.
* **Yonge–Dundas 1 / 1** with 87 IDs for a busy 15 s crossing: plausible under-count (wide shot, small
  people, many short tracks), no ground truth to say more.
* Track IDs on the page (all IDs drawn, e.g. 167) differ from the backend `metrics.tracks` (IDs with ≥ 3
  frames, 136 on Shibuya). Both are honest but differently defined (P2).
* First UI run of `pexels-subway` hung: I edited a web file mid-run and Vite hot-reload remounted the
  page, dropping the polling loop (backend job had succeeded). Re-run clean. Not a product bug, but
  the page does not resume a running job after reload (P2).
